Who We Are

Malkiyat Constructions

Malkiyat Constructions (Malkiyat Associates & Builders) is a PEC registered firm based at B Block, Gulberg Greens, Islamabad. We build residential homes across Islamabad and Rawalpindi, handling the complete journey: design, grey structure, and finishing.

Every project is personally supervised by our contractor, Sarmad Awan, and clients receive stage-by-stage updates, from foundation to final paint. Our promise is simple: the quality we commit on paper is the quality standing on your plot.
