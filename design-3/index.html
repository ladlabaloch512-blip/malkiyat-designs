About us

One team, from the first drawing to the last light fitting

Malkiyat Associates & Builders is a construction firm based in Gulberg Greens, Islamabad. We build homes in Islamabad and Rawalpindi and take each project from design to finishing under one team, so the family building the house deals with one office from start to end.

We are a PEC registered firm and a member of the Islamabad Chamber of Commerce. Every house is priced before the work begins, and the written quotation follows a site visit and approved drawings.

How we build

From plot to keys, in five clear steps

01

Site visit and estimate

We visit your plot, listen to what you want, and give a first estimate from the same rates as the calculator on this page.

02

Design and written quotation

Drawings are prepared for your plot and you receive a written quotation listing the package, covered area and main materials.

03

Grey structure

The house is cleaned, checked with you room by room, and handed over ready to live in.
