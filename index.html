What We Do

Complete Construction Services

From the first drawing to the final key handover, one team handles your complete project.

Grey Structure Construction

Complete grey structure built to engineering standards: excavation, foundation, columns, beams, slabs and brickwork, with quality material and proper curing at every stage.

Turnkey Construction & Finishing

We hand over a ready-to-move home. Flooring, woodwork, kitchens, bathrooms, paint, ceiling and fixtures, finished neatly and checked room by room before handover.

Design & Planning

Architectural drawings, floor plans and 3D views planned around your plot, your family needs and your budget, before a single brick is laid.

Renovation & Remodelling

Old homes brought back to life: structural repairs, new finishes, kitchen and bathroom upgrades, and full interior remodelling.

Cost Estimation & Packages

Clear per-square-foot packages with no hidden charges. Use our online calculator for an instant estimate, then get a detailed written quotation.

Project Supervision

Stage-by-stage supervision and progress updates, material checks and workmanship inspection, so your project stays on time and on standard.

About Malkiyat

From Grey Structure to a Finished Home

Malkiyat Constructions is a PEC registered construction firm based in Gulberg Greens, Islamabad, building homes across Islamabad and Rawalpindi. Our project banners say it simply: Design, Built, Finishing, complete construction services under one roof.
