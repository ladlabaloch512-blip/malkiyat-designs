About us

A construction firm in Gulberg Greens, Islamabad.

Malkiyat Associates & Builders builds homes in Islamabad and Rawalpindi, from the first drawings to the last coat of paint. One team takes your house through design, grey structure and finishing, so the people who draw it are answerable for the people who build it.

We are a PEC registered firm and a member of the Islamabad Chamber of Commerce. Our office is in B Block, Gulberg Greens, Islamabad, and our contractor Sarmad Awan oversees each project personally.

Services

Complete construction services.

Design
