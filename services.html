Our Services

Complete construction services: design, build and finishing, with clear packages and honest pricing.

Grey Structure Construction

The skeleton of your home, built right: excavation and foundation, RCC columns and beams, roof slabs, brick masonry and plaster base. We follow the structural drawing exactly, use standard cement and steel, and cure every stage properly.

  • Foundation and RCC frame as per structural design
  • Brick masonry with standard material
  • Roof slabs, stairs and mumty structure
  • Underground and overhead water tanks

Turnkey Construction & Finishing

One contract, one team, a ready-to-move home. After grey structure we complete flooring, false ceilings, woodwork, modular kitchen, bathrooms, paint, electrical and sanitary finishing, and hand over the keys.

  • Flooring, tiles and marble work
  • Kitchen, wardrobes and woodwork
  • Paint, polish and false ceiling
  • Electrical, sanitary and fixture fitting

Design & Planning

Good homes are decided on paper first. Our design service covers floor plans that respect your plot dimensions and bylaws, a front elevation you will be proud of, and working drawings the site team can build from without guesswork.
