About us

A construction firm you can check

Malkiyat Associates & Builders is a construction firm based in B Block, Gulberg Greens, Islamabad. We build homes in Islamabad and Rawalpindi, and we take the job from the first drawing to the day we hand over the keys.

Every house is priced before the first brick is laid. The estimate on this page uses the same rates our team quotes from, so the figure you see online is the figure we start the conversation with. After a site visit and drawings, you receive a written quotation.

You decide how much of the job we take on. Grey structure covers the foundation, columns, beams, slabs and brickwork, and you arrange the finishing yourself. Turnkey construction means we hand over a finished house, and the package you choose sets the grade of the finishing materials.

How we work

From plot to keys, in five steps

  1. 1

    Site visit and survey

    We visit your plot, take measurements and talk through what you want to build.

  2. 2

    Drawings and written quote

    Plans and elevations are drawn, and you receive a written quotation before you commit.

  3. 3

    Foundation and grey structure

    Footings, columns, beams, slabs and brickwork go up floor by floor.

  4. 4

    Finishing

    Plaster, cladding, glazing, woodwork and lighting, to the package you chose.

  5. 5

    Keys in hand

    The house is cleaned, checked with you on site, and handed over ready to live in.
